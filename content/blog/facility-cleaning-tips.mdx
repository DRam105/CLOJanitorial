A clean workplace isn't just about appearances — it affects employee health, productivity, and the impression you make on every client who walks through the door. Here are seven practical tips to keep your facility looking its best between professional cleanings.

## 1. Focus on high-touch points daily

Door handles, light switches, elevator buttons, shared keyboards and breakroom appliances are germ hot-spots. A quick daily wipe-down with an EPA-registered disinfectant dramatically reduces the spread of illness.

## 2. Keep entryways clean

Up to 80% of the dirt in a building is tracked in on shoes. Quality entrance mats and regular vacuuming at doorways protect your floors and cut down on cleaning everywhere else.

## 3. Don't neglect restrooms

Nothing shapes perception of a facility faster than its restrooms. Keep them stocked, sanitized and checked throughout the day — especially in high-traffic buildings.

## 4. Address spills immediately

Spills on carpet or hard floors become stains and slip hazards fast. A simple spill-response kit and clear staff guidance prevents bigger problems.

## 5. Improve indoor air quality

Dust surfaces regularly, replace HVAC filters on schedule, and use HEPA-filtration vacuums. Better air quality means fewer sick days and a fresher space.

## 6. Create a cleaning checklist

Consistency comes from a documented scope. A simple checklist ensures nothing gets missed — and makes it easy to hold any provider accountable.

## 7. Partner with professionals

Daily upkeep helps, but deep cleaning, floor care and disinfection require trained crews and the right equipment. A reliable janitorial partner keeps your facility consistently spotless — so you can focus on your business.
